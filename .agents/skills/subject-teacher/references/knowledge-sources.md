## 📐 Mathematical Foundations for AI

### 📖 Primary Textbook
- **Mathematics for Machine Learning** (Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong)
  - 🆓 Free PDF: https://mml-book.github.io/
  - Chapter map: Ch.2=Linear Algebra, Ch.3=Analytic Geometry, Ch.4=Matrix Decomp, Ch.5=Vector Calculus, Ch.6=Probability, Ch.7=Optimization
- **Gilbert Strang — Introduction to Linear Algebra (5th Ed)**
  - The original textbook. Pairs perfectly with his MIT OCW lectures.

### 🎛️ YouTube — Linear Algebra
- **3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra** (BEST for intuition)
  - Playlist: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDPD3MizzM2xVFitgF8hE_ab
  - Chapter 1 (Vectors): https://youtu.be/fNk_zzaMoSs
  - Chapter 3 (Linear transformations): https://youtu.be/kYB8IZa5AuE
  - Chapter 6 (Cross products): https://youtu.be/eu6i7WJeinw
  - Chapter 13 (Change of basis): https://youtu.be/P2LTAUO1TdA
  - Chapter 14 (Eigenvectors): https://youtu.be/PFDu9oVAE-g
- **MIT 18.06 — Linear Algebra (Gilbert Strang himself)**
  - Full course: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/
  - Best academic source for rigorous LA

### 🎛️ YouTube — Probability & Statistics
- **StatQuest with Josh Starmer** (BEST for intuitive stats)
  - Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@statquest
  - Probability basics: https://youtu.be/uzkc-qNVoOk
  - Bayes theorem: https://youtu.be/9wCnvr7Xw4I
  - MLE: https://youtu.be/XepXtl9YKwc
  - Normal distribution: https://youtu.be/rzFX5NWojp0
  - Information Theory / Entropy: https://youtu.be/YtebGVx-Fxw

### 🌐 NPTEL

  - 12 weeks, 60 lectures, exam-aligned
- **Probability and Statistics — IIT Bombay**
  - Link: https://nptel.ac.in/courses/108101002

### 🧪 Practice / Coding

- 3Blue1Brown's interactive linear algebra: https://eater.net/linear-algebra/

## 📊 Computational Optimization

### 📖 Primary Textbook
- **Convex Optimization** (Boyd & Vandenberghe)
  - 🆓 Free PDF: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/
  - THE definitive reference. Ch.1-4 = theory, Ch.5-6 = algorithms
- **Numerical Optimization** (Nocedal & Wright 2nd Ed)
