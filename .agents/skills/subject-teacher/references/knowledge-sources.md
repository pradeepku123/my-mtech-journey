## 📐 Mathematical Foundations for AI

### 📖 Primary Textbook
- **Mathematics for Machine Learning** (Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong)
  - 🆓 Free PDF: https://mml-book.github.io/
  - Chapter map: Ch.2=Linear Algebra, Ch.3=Analytic Geometry, Ch.4=Matrix Decomp, Ch.5=Vector Calculus, Ch.6=Probability, Ch.7=Optimization

  - Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@statquest

## 📊 Computational Optimization

### 📖 Primary Textbook
- **Convex Optimization** (Boyd & Vandenberghe)
  - 🆓 Free PDF: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/
  - THE definitive reference. Ch.1-4 = theory, Ch.5-6 = algorithms
- **Numerical Optimization** (Nocedal & Wright 2nd Ed)
  - More algorithm-focused, good for second-order methods

### 🎬 YouTube
- **Stanford EE364A — Convex Optimization** (Stephen Boyd himself)
  - Full course: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL3940DD956CDF0622
- **Gradient Descent variants** (Sebastian Ruder's illustrated guide)
  - Blog: https://www.ruder.io/optimizing-gradient-descent/
- **Andrej Karpathy — micrograd** (autograd from scratch)
  - https://youtu.be/VMj-3S1tku0

### 🌐 NPTEL
- **Optimization — IIT Kharagpur**
  - https://nptel.ac.in/courses/106105085
- **Convex Optimization — IIT Bombay**
  - https://nptel.ac.in/courses/108100085

### 📄 Key Papers
- "Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization" (Kingma & Ba 2015) — most cited ML paper
- "An Overview of Gradient Descent Optimization Algorithms" (Ruder 2016)
