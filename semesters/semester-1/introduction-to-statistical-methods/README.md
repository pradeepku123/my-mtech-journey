# 📊 Introduction to Statistical Methods

> **Course Code**: Core 2 | **Semester**: 1  
> **Institution**: BITS Pilani WILP (M.Tech AIML)

---

## 📖 Course Overview

This course provides fundamental probabilistic and statistical reasoning concepts required for Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, including probability distributions, estimation theory, hypothesis testing, and Bayesian inferential methods.

---

## 📋 Syllabus & Topic Checklist

- [ ] **Probability & Random Variables**: Sample space, events, probability axioms, conditional probability, Bayes' Rule
- [ ] **Probability Distributions**: Discrete (Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson) and Continuous (Uniform, Normal/Gaussian, Exponential)
- [ ] **Joint & Marginal Distributions**: Bivariate distributions, covariance, correlation, independence
- [ ] **Sampling Distributions & CLT**: Central Limit Theorem, sample mean/variance distributions
- [ ] **Point & Interval Estimation**: Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE), Method of Moments, Confidence Intervals
- [ ] **Hypothesis Testing**: Null & Alternative hypotheses, p-values, t-tests, z-tests, Chi-square tests, ANOVA
- [ ] **Bayesian Inference**: Prior, Likelihood, Posterior, Conjugate priors, Maximum A Posteriori (MAP) estimation
